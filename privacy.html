2. General principles

For the app, the aim is to create as little data as possible: there is no user account and no cloud storage. All app data (dog profiles, walk history, settings) stays exclusively on your device. As the provider, we have no access to this data — it never reaches us.

For some app functions (weather data, map display, place search) the app sends location data in real time to specialised third-party providers — details in section 4.

In addition, the website pawsafe.eu runs a simple server for the waiting list (section 3a) and, with your consent, uses Google Analytics to measure reach (section 3b).

3a. Waiting list on pawsafe.eu

If you sign up for the waiting list via the form on pawsafe.eu, we process your email address in order to inform you about the app launch. The request runs through our server (api.pawsafe.eu) and is delivered to us by email and additionally stored in a backup file on the server.

  • Purpose: Notification about the availability of the app
  • Legal basis: Consent (Art. 6(1)(a) GDPR) by actively submitting the form
  • Recipients: Only us, no disclosure to third parties
  • Storage location: Server in Germany (IONOS), no transfer to third countries
  • Storage period: Until the app launch or until you object to the storage
  • Withdrawal: At any time, informally, by email to info@pawsafe.eu

3b. Reach measurement on pawsafe.eu (Google Analytics)

The website pawsafe.eu uses Google Analytics, a web analytics service of Google Ireland Limited, to understand how the site is used (e.g. page views, clicks on "Get early access"). The script loads only if you actively consent in the cookie banner on your first visit — without consent no Google script is loaded and no analytics cookie is set.

  • Purpose: Reach measurement, improving the website
  • Data collected: including shortened IP address, device type, browser, pages visited, click events
  • Legal basis: Consent (Art. 6(1)(a) GDPR, Section 25(1) TDDDG)
  • Recipients: Google Ireland Limited; transfer to the USA is possible (Google is certified under the EU-US Data Privacy Framework)
  • Withdrawal: Via the cookie banner (it appears again if you delete your local browser data for this site) or via the Google opt-out add-on
  • More information: Google's privacy policy

4. Location data and disclosure to third-party providers (app)

PawSafe asks for your device's location permission in order to provide ground temperature, safety assessment and map functions. During active walk tracking, background location permission is additionally required so that the route keeps being recorded while the screen is locked. The recorded route stays on your device and is not transmitted to third parties.

For the following functions, coordinates (latitude/longitude) and in part search input are transmitted live to external services — without being linked to a name, account or other user identifier. The app triggers the transmission as soon as you use the respective function (e.g. when opening the home screen, the forecast or the map). For technical reasons the providers also receive your IP address.

Legal basis: Transmitting your location is based on your consent, which you give by granting location permission in the operating system (Art. 6(1)(a) GDPR). You can withdraw it at any time in the device settings; the lawfulness of processing carried out until then remains unaffected. We transmit search input so that the search you triggered works (Art. 6(1)(b) GDPR).

No obligation to provide data: Location permission and search input are voluntary. Without location permission, functions that need your current location cannot be used or only to a limited extent.

No automated decision-making: The safety assessments (traffic light, recommendations) are calculated on your device from weather data and your dog details. No decision within the meaning of Art. 22 GDPR takes place.

The providers named process the requests they receive under their own responsibility (including the technically necessary processing of the IP address when the connection is established). For enquiries about this processing, please contact the respective provider directly.

6. Push notifications

Notifications are generated locally on your device (currently only the status of a running walk). There is no push server of our own and no transmission of notification content to us or to third parties. Should we offer further notifications in the future, we will update this policy beforehand.

7. No tracking, no advertising, no analytics in the app

The PawSafe app contains no analytics SDK, no crash reporting, no advertising networks and no tracking mechanisms. There is no profiling and no sale of data. (The reach measurement described in section 3b applies to the website only, not to the app.)

App updates: When the app starts, it checks via the update service Expo Application Services (EAS Update) of Expo (650 Industries, Inc., USA) whether a new version of the app software is available. For technical reasons this transmits your IP address, the app version, platform / operating system and a randomly generated identifier for the update request — no location data and no content of your app data. The legal basis is our legitimate interest in a secure and up-to-date app (Art. 6(1)(f) GDPR); you can object to this under Art. 21 GDPR. According to its own statements, Expo is certified under the EU-US Data Privacy Framework.

The app is downloaded and installed via the Apple App Store or Google Play. Apple and Google process data under their own responsibility in that context; we have no influence on this.

8. Storage period

Local data (dogs, walks, places, settings) remains stored until you delete it yourself or uninstall the app. For location data transmitted live to third-party providers, their respective retention periods apply — see the privacy information linked in section 4.

9. Your rights

Because we, as the controller, have no access to your app data, most data-subject rights (access, rectification, erasure) can be exercised directly in the app — the data is on your device. For your waiting-list email (section 3a), the website analytics (section 3b) and for any other requests you can reach us at info@pawsafe.eu — there you can request access, rectification, erasure or withdrawal of your consent.

Under the GDPR you have in particular the following rights: access (Art. 15), rectification (Art. 16), erasure (Art. 17), restriction of processing (Art. 18), data portability (Art. 20), objection to processing based on legitimate interests (Art. 21), and withdrawal of consent given, with effect for the future (Art. 7(3)). You may also lodge a complaint with a data-protection supervisory authority (Art. 77 GDPR), e.g. the authority responsible for us, the State Commissioner for Data Protection and Freedom of Information of North Rhine-Westphalia (ldi.nrw.de).

10. Minors

PawSafe is not specifically aimed at children and does not collect age-specific information.

11. Changes to this policy

This policy may be updated as the app develops. The current version is available via the app and pawsafe.eu.
